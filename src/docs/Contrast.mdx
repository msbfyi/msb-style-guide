# The Contrast Principle

<blockquote style={{ borderLeft: "5px solid #B71C2A", paddingLeft: "20px", fontSize: "22px", fontWeight: 700, color: "#1B2A52" }}>
  Contrast is the whole language. Two things, hard boundary, no
  blending.
</blockquote>

## Rules

**i.** Pair a saturated accent with a neutral, never two saturated
accents against each other. Energy-on-Dramatic reads as noise, not
contrast.

**ii.** Every ornament and pattern must be a *closed shape with an end
point* — a repeat that resolves, not a texture that trails off.

**iii.** Metals are a flourish, never a field. Gold or silver as a
background is a design smell.

**iv.** When in doubt, go flatter and harder-edged, not softer. There
are no gradients and no drop shadows anywhere in this system.
