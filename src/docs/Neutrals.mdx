Three neutrals stand in for the usual black/brown/white, plus two

## Metals

Silver (`--silver`, `#C0C0C0`) and Gold (`--gold`, `#BFA060`) — used as
accents only, never as a solo fill. Gold in particular never carries
text alone; it pairs with a neutral.

## Ideal ratio

Roughly two parts neutral to one part accent color, with metals as the
final 5–10% flourish — not the other way around.
