# Light & Dark

The palette does not change in the dark theme; the *roles* do. Six
semantic tokens swap, and the two colors that carry text — Energy and
Romantic — hand off to Essence and Gold, which hold their contrast on
navy.

## Rules

**i. Cobalt stays cobalt.** The dispatch plate (`<msb-dispatch>`) is
Dramatic in both themes — only its keyline changes, to optic white at
42%. A color plate is a fixed field, not a themed surface. This is why
`--plate-stroke` exists as its own token rather than reusing `--hair-c`.

**ii. Accents follow the link.** Anything Romantic that carries
meaning goes Essence in the dark theme. Romantic on navy is the one
pairing that fails.

**iii. System preference is the default.** Auto follows the OS; Light
and Dark are explicit and persist. Three segments (`<msb-mode-toggle>`),
never a two-state switch.

**iv. Measured, not assumed.** Navy on paper 12.5:1 · Romantic on
paper 6.0:1 · Energy on paper 4.6:1 · optic white on navy 12.5:1 ·
Essence on navy 7.6:1 · Gold on navy 5.5:1. Every text pairing clears
WCAG AA at body size.

## How it's implemented

`tokens.css` defines the light values on `:root`, swaps them under
`@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)`, and lets an explicit
`[data-theme="light"]`/`[data-theme="dark"]` attribute on `<html>`
override either direction — that's exactly what `<msb-mode-toggle>`
manages for you by default.
